One 1024×1024 image summing up **Wednesday 26 November 2026** — the day's
20 strongest stories, verified story by story against first-report dates.

R2 is tighter and bolder than R1: two large hero panels (Venezuela bridge
loans, Krasnodar refinery fire), a clean 3×6 grid of 18 more stories with
big readable headlines, and a slim timeline strip of five verified times.
Every story was first reported on 26 November 2026.

## How it works

`render.py` (PIL, render time only) draws `entry.png`. `main.py` is
stdlib-only and just copies `entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`.
