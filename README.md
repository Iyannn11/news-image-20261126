A single 1024x1024 image summing up world news from **Thursday 26 November 2026** — 29 stories, each verified against its own dated source.

[Bounty page](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261126)

## What's inside

- **Top stories:** Trump's new tariffs, the drone strike in Kuwait, Nvidia halting AI shipments to Southeast Asia
- **World map:** 13 numbered pins marking where the news happened
- **Flags of the day:** 10 countries behind the biggest stories
- **UTC timeline:** 6 timed events from the day
- **All 29 stories:** one short line each, all from 26 November 2026 only

## Files

- `entry.png` — the submitted image (1024x1024, under 1 MiB)
- `main.py` — stdlib-only entry point: copies `entry.png` to `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`
- `render.py` — draws `entry.png` with Pillow (local rendering only)
